The Indian E2W market features a mix of established OEMs and fast-growing startups. Key players include:

**Ola Electric**: Market leader with ~35% market share in FY2024. Vertically integrated, operating the world's largest E2V factory (Futurefactory, Tamil Nadu). Revenue FY2024: INR 5,243 crore. Losses remain high at INR 1,585 crore due to heavy R&D and capex. Recently launched the S1 X at INR 79,999 to capture mass market.

**Hero MotoCorp (Vida brand)**: Leveraging established distribution network of 6,000+ dealers. Vida V1 priced at INR 1.4–1.7 lakh. Partnership with Ather for technology transfer. FY2024 E2W sales: ~180,000 units.

**Ather Energy**: Premium segment, backed by Hero MotoCorp and Tiger Global. Strong software integration (Ather Grid charging network). Planned IPO in 2025. FY2024 revenue: INR 1,753 crore. Known for superior NPS (78) among E2W brands.

**TVS Motor (iQube)**: FY2024 E2W sales ~280,000 units. Part of broader ICE product family strategy. iQube variants range INR 94,000–1.6 lakh.

**Bajaj Auto (Chetak)**: Heritage brand revival. Pan-India expansion underway; FY2024 sales ~195,000 units. Manufacturing at Pune and Chakan facilities.

**Emerging players**: Revolt Motors (motorcycles, subscription model), Greaves Electric (Ampere brand, rural focus), Matter (premium segment, collaboration with Bharat Forge for motors).

International entrants: Yamaha (EMF motorcycle in pipeline), Honda Activa Electric (launched late 2024, INR 1.1 lakh), Suzuki Avenis E (expected 2025).

Competitive dynamics: price pressure intensifying in entry segment (sub-INR 1 lakh); differentiation shifting to software, fleet B2B, and battery-as-a-service models.
