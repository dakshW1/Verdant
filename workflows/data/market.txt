India's electric two-wheeler (E2W) market is experiencing rapid growth, driven by rising fuel prices, government incentives, and increasing environmental awareness. The market was valued at approximately USD 1.8 billion in 2023 and is projected to grow at a CAGR of 22% through 2030, reaching an estimated USD 7.5 billion.

Key market segments include electric scooters (60% market share), electric motorcycles (30%), and electric mopeds (10%). Urban adoption is concentrated in tier-1 and tier-2 cities, with Delhi, Bangalore, and Hyderabad leading in registrations.

The FAME II scheme (Faster Adoption and Manufacturing of Electric Vehicles) provided subsidies of up to INR 15,000 per kWh, catalyzing OEM investment. Approximately 1.3 million E2Ws were sold in FY2024, up from 780,000 in FY2023.

Battery costs have declined from USD 450/kWh in 2020 to USD 185/kWh in 2024, making entry-level E2Ws price-competitive with ICE equivalents on a total cost of ownership basis. Charging infrastructure remains a challenge, with approximately 12,000 public charging stations as of 2024 versus an estimated need of 400,000 by 2030.

Consumer surveys indicate range anxiety (cited by 68% of non-adopters) and charging time (cited by 54%) as the primary barriers to purchase. Battery swap networks, pioneered by companies like Gogoro and Battery Smart, are gaining traction as a solution.
